About Us

Who we are, what we do, and how to get in touch.

Nobody in Scotland should go hungry

FoodForScotland brings together foodbanks, volunteers, local businesses and neighbours. We make it easy to see what is needed, give what you can, and find a meal when you need one.

Our mission

To tackle hunger in Scotland by making it simple to donate, volunteer and get food.

What we do

Show what is needed

A live counter of the food groups foodbanks are running low on.

Organise volunteers

People deliver food, cook hot meals, and companies lend vans and cars.

Listen to ideas

Anyone can suggest ways to improve the foodbank or help in new ways.

Why it matters

Trussell's Scottish foodbanks handed out 262,400 emergency food parcels in 2023/24, close to the highest on record.
